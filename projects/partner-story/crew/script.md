# Script · Partner Story

Footage job, Enablement DS business track. **No voice-over and no `vo.json`.** Every spoken word is Samra's own
(Egyptian Arabic), cut from the interview clips. The captions are the text below, as said.

How this was checked: each beat's range was cut out and re-transcribed on its own with whisper large-v3-turbo, twice
(plain, then with a vocabulary prompt for Speaking Genie, session, feedback, AI, goal, practice, LLM, ChatGPT,
judgement). The token timings in `build/transcripts/*.json` were then used to confirm where each in and out falls.
`[?]` marks a word that is still uncertain after these passes. Please listen to those words before Gate 2.

**Caption spelling rules.** Write words as he says them, in Egyptian spelling (بيطلّع، إزاي، دلوقتي). Put English
words he uses as English in Latin script (Speaking Genie, AI, LLM, ChatGPT, session, feedback, goal, practice,
judgement, courses, English, grammar, solution). Loanwords that are fully Arabised stay in Arabic (الجيم، الموديل).
Keep the Arabic article attached to a Latin word (الـfeedback، الـAI). Line breaks below are the caption chunks,
so each chunk is a single burned-in caption (about 4–7 words). Captions get no exclamation marks and no emoji.
