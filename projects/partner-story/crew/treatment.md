# Treatment · Partner Story

**Logline.** A founder who studied English for years and still froze when he spoke built the practice he needed, and partners can now bring it to the centres and companies they know.

**Viewer and job.** Partners who know centre owners, corporates, L&D and HR. They get it forwarded on WhatsApp or by email and watch it once, often muted. After 2 minutes they can say in one sentence what Speaking Genie is ("speaking practice with AI, not another course"), and they get in touch about a referral.

**System and track.** Enablement DS, **business track**. Orange accent (`#ff8e31` for the phrase on purple), purple dome grounds, one capsule per frame, Cairo for Arabic type. On-screen Arabic is simplified formal Arabic. Samra speaks Egyptian colloquial because that is his real voice. The video closes on a conversation, never an install, with no store badges and no prices. Product screens are shown as shipped: mobile kit for the learner side, web kit for the centre side.

**Shape.** One cut, 9:16, 1080×1920, `reels-1080x1920`, target 2:00 (±5 s after silence trims). Arabic (`lang: ar`). Burned-in Arabic captions of his exact words.

**Takes.** For "why I started" I chose clip 11 over clip 10. It is cleaner, it names Speaking Genie and it states the confidence gap outright. Clip 10's ending catches him looking off-camera with a hand over his mouth.

**Not used:**
- Clip 13 (store install) and clip 2's prices (700–2,000 EGP, which conflicts with the brief's 700–1,200) are left out on both track rules.
- Clip 12's "after 15 minutes / one month / three months" promises are outcome claims with no method behind them.
- Clips 1, 3, 5, 6 and 8 are also left out. They fall back to the editor if B-roll or trims need them. Clip 1, 11.00–26.00 ("Speaking Genie doesn't teach, it's practice") can swap in for B7.

## Look and sound
- **Footage.** Five setups alternate: window desk (11, 12), grey sofa (2, 7), ceiling room (4), plant (9). Every cut changes setup except inside B1–B5, where punch-ins of 1.0 → 1.15 hide the two jumps. Clips 11 and 12 are backlit: lift the faces, and watch the moiré on the clip 12 blinds.
- **Joins.** Mostly cuts on the first stressed word. Use `dip` into B6 and into B14, and `push-right` at B7, the first product beat. Leave the rest as cuts.
- **Text and screens.** Screens get a living camera with `drift` 0.03 and one callout each. Callout labels name the verbatim feature: Post-session feedback, Weekly and monthly reports, Curriculum builder. Keep text out of the bottom 20%.
- **Captions.** Word-by-word Arabic, burned in, written as spoken: colloquial, with product terms in Latin (session, feedback, AI, goal).
- **LowerThird.** At B1 + 0.6 s for 4 s: «أحمد أبوسمرة» · «Co-founder, Speaking Genie».
- **Sound.** An understated corporate bed if the user supplies one, ducked under the voice. `sfx` is quiet (0.3). The logo is a small white lockup, hidden on motion clips.
