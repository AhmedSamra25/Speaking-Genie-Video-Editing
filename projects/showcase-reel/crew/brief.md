# Brief · Showcase reel

- **Goal:** prove the capabilities plan (phases 1–5 plus rack focus and speed ramp) in one finished piece, and serve as the
  after-benchmark for render time and memory. Internal proof piece, not a campaign; nothing ships from it unreviewed.
- **Audience:** language-centre owners and managers. **Design system:** Enablement DS, **track: business** (orange
  accent, third person about "your students / your centre", close on a conversation, never store badges).
- **Platforms and cuts:** Arabic 9:16 (`reels-1080x1920`, this project) and English 16:9 (`youtube-1080p`,
  `projects/showcase-reel-en`), ~45 s. Two cuts sharing one treatment.
- **Language:** simplified formal Arabic (voice and screen), English for the 16:9 cut.
- **Voice:** SCRATCH. macOS `say` (Majed Premium for Arabic at 280 wpm, Ava Premium for English at 190 wpm). A timing
  placeholder only: MSA with a Gulf accent, synthetic. Replace with a recorded or cleared voice before any release.
- **Supplied:** partner-story footage (Ahmed Abousamra, clip 11), real screens from both kits, the brand sound library.
- **Must use:** screen-to-device zoom, tilt float, element travel and cursor, DualScreen, parallax, voice bars, the
  dome reveal, zoom/push/slide-scale/blur/dip, TransitionSeries runs, line-mask titles, marker, capsule wipe,
  Arabic-Indic digits, the light sweep, rack focus, brand sound cues and a licensed music bed.
