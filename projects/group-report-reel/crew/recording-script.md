# Recording script · Group report Reel

Eight lines, Egyptian Arabic. Read them in order, **one take**, with a **2-second pause** between lines.

## The lines

| # | Say this | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | تعرف طلابك بيغلطوا في إيه؟ | A real question, curious, not accusing. Lift on «إيه». |
| 2 | أكتر من نص الجروب، نفس الغلطة. | Small pause after «الجروب», then «نفس الغلطة» calm and sure. |
| 3 | ده الجروب ريبورت: الفصل كله في صورة واحدة. | Like showing something you're proud of. Stress «كله». |
| 4 | مين اتمرّن وقد إيه، مترتبين على نفس الأسبوع. | Even pace, small pause at the comma. |
| 5 | جمل حقيقية قالها الطلاب، ومعاها تِب للحصة الجاية. | Warmer. Stress «حقيقية». |
| 6 | بيوصلك لوحده، بي دي إف تبعته للأهالي والعملاء. | The payoff. Stress «لوحده». |
| 7 | وجودة التدريس، تشوفها كل أسبوع. | Quiet and certain. Stress «تشوفها». |
| 8 | ابدأ تجربة مجانية النهارده. | An invitation, not a push. Smile on «مجانية», end with your voice going down. |

English terms the way Egyptian teachers say them in class: **group** = جروب · **report** = ريبورت · **tip** = تِب · **PDF** = بي دي إف.

## Setup (5 minutes)

- A small quiet room with soft things around (curtains, a bed, a sofa). Turn off the AC and fans; put the phone on Do Not Disturb.
- Voice Memos on your iPhone. Hold the phone a hand-width from your mouth, slightly to the side so your breath doesn't hit the mic. Keep the same distance for every line.
- Record 3 seconds of a test line and play it back on headphones: no echo, no hum.

## How to read

- Calm and warm, one centre owner telling another about something that works. Not an ad, not a news read.
- **Read at your natural pace. Don't rush to fit a time.** The video is cut to your voice; if a line comes out long, we trim words, not your delivery.
- If you trip on a line, pause and say that line again; the last take of a line is the one used. (Then tell me which line you repeated, or record the whole set again.)
- Say the eight lines exactly as written. If you change a word, tell me: the captions are written from these lines.

## Send it

Save as **M4A** (Voice Memos → Share → Save to Files) or WAV, put it in

I'll clean it up (noise, room rumble, harsh "s", level), cut it into the eight lines, fit the picture and the captions
to your read, and render the final.
