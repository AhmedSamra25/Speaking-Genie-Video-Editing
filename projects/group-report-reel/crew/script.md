## Recording notes (for you, on your phone)

**Setup**
- A small quiet room with soft things around (a bedroom with curtains and a bed is better than a tiled kitchen). Turn off the AC and fans; phone on airplane mode so nothing buzzes.
- Use the Voice Memos app (or any recorder). Hold the phone about a hand-width from your mouth, a little to the side so your breath doesn't hit the mic. Keep the same distance for every line.
- Record a 3-second test, play it back on headphones, and check there's no echo or hum.

**How to read**
- Calm and warm, like telling another centre owner about something that works for you. Not an ad, not a news read. Smile slightly; it shows in the voice.
- Say each line once, wait two seconds, then the next one. If you trip, just say the line again; I'll keep the best take. Say L1 to L8 in order, then record the whole set a second time.
- Don't rush to fit. If a line feels long, tell me and I'll cut words.

**Words to stress**
- L1 "إيه" (a real question) · L2 pause after "الـgroup", then "نفس الغلطة" · L3 "كله" · L5 "حقيقية" · L6 "لوحده" · L7 "تشوفها" · L8 "مجانية", ending down, not up.

**Product terms** (the way Egyptian teachers say them in class, no heavy American accent)
